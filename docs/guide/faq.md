## Is OpenVFR approved for navigation?

No. It is for planning and secondary situational awareness only. See the
notice on the [start page](./index.md).

## Is it free?

Yes. It is open source (MIT) with no subscription and no paywalls.

## Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign in with a passkey or an email code. Weather, NOTAMs, traffic and
sync go through the OpenVFR server, which needs a signed-in account. There
is no password and no payment. See [Account and sync](./account-and-sync.md).

## Which countries are covered?

Sweden first, then the Nordics and other European countries.

## Where do I report a problem or contribute?

See the project's GitHub repository.
