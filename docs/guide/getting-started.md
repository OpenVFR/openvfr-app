## Open the app

OpenVFR runs in the browser as a progressive web app (PWA), and there is an
Android and iOS app. The web app can be installed from your browser's
"Install" or "Add to Home Screen" menu, after which it also works offline
for data you have already loaded.

## The basics

- The map shows aviation layers on top of the basemap. See
  [Map and layers](./map-and-layers.md).
- Tap or click features (aerodromes, airspaces, navaids, obstacles) to see
  details.
- Build a route by adding waypoints on the map. See
  [Planning a route](./planning-a-route.md).
- Turn on flying mode to follow your GPS position and get alerts. See
  [Flying mode and alerts](./flying-and-alerts.md).

## Accounts and sync

An account is optional. Without one, your routes, aircraft and settings are
stored on your device. With one, they can be synced between devices.
