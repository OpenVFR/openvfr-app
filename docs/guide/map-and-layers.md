The map combines a basemap with aviation layers: airspace, aerodromes,
runways, navaids, waypoints, obstacles and visual landmarks.

## Airspace

- Airspace is coloured by class and drawn with a shaded band on the inside
  of its boundary, so you can tell which side is the airspace.
- Limits are shown as published. A limit given as AGL or GND is a height
  above ground, not an altitude, so compare it with your height above
  ground, not your altitude above sea level.
- The altitude slider sets a ceiling: airspace that starts above it is
  hidden. When your GPS altitude climbs within 500 ft of the ceiling in
  flying mode, the ceiling is raised by 2,000 ft and you are notified, so
  the airspace you are about to enter is not hidden.

## Layer panel

Open the layer panel to switch layer groups on and off.

## Finding things

Use search to find aerodromes, navaids and other features by name.
