One line per feature, grouped as in the app. For which platform has what,
see [Web app and native app](./platforms.md).

## Map

- Layers: airspace by class, aerodromes, runways, navaids, waypoints,
  obstacles, landmarks, your saved places. See [Map and layers](./map-and-layers.md).
- Terrain: land use, hillshade, contour lines, colour bands.
- Wind arrows and live air traffic.
- Airspace ceiling slider.
- Search for aerodromes and features, and share links.

## Planning

- Route building with snapping, undo and redo.
- Leg heading, wind correction, time, fuel. See [Planning a route](./planning-a-route.md).
- Forecast and manual wind, takeoff time, alternate.
- Aircraft profiles and fuel plan.
- Pre-flight checks: airspace, minimum safe altitude, fuel.
- Saved routes and saved places.
- Find destination by fuel, surface and runway length.
- Vertical profile of the route.

## Weather and NOTAMs

- METAR, TAF and flight category. See [Weather and NOTAMs](./weather-and-notams.md).
- Aerodrome brief and nearby brief.
- NOTAMs on the map and per aerodrome.
- Winds aloft, cloud profile, wind gauges.

## Flying

- Flying mode, auto start and stop, direct to, auto zoom. See [Flying mode and alerts](./flying-and-alerts.md).
- Alert stack for airspace, obstacles and aerodromes.
- Drop a point, pilot log, simulators.
- Flight logs, review and GPX. See [Flight logs](./flight-logs.md).
- Native only: background tracking, barometer, vario, QNH handling,
  instrument readouts.

## Account

- Passkey and email code sign-in, optional.
- Sync across devices.
