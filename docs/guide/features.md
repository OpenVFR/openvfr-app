One line per feature, grouped as in the app. For which platform has what,
see [Web app and native app](./platforms.md).

## Map
- Basemap: vector, satellite (planning only). [Map and layers](./map-and-layers.md)
- Airspace by class, with a ceiling slider. [Map and layers](./map-and-layers.md)
- Aerodromes, runways, navaids, reporting points, VFR waypoints, obstacles, landmarks, your places.
- Terrain: land use, hillshade, contour lines, terrain colour.
- Wind arrows. Air traffic. [Air traffic](./air-traffic.md)
- Search by name, coordinate or bearing and distance, and share links.
- Ruler, compass and orientation, map info bar and credits.
- What's here (web) and the tap popover (native).

## Planning
- Build a route by tapping, searching, typing or importing. [Planning a route](./planning-a-route.md)
- Snapping, drag to edit, undo and redo, reverse.
- Route table: tracks, heading, wind correction, level, time. [Planning a route](./planning-a-route.md)
- Leg properties: level, speed, wind, notes, MSA.
- Takeoff time, forecast or manual wind, alternate.
- Automatic cruise level and pre-flight checks.
- Saved routes, GPX and KML. [Saved routes and places](./routes-and-places.md)
- Places and folders. [Saved routes and places](./routes-and-places.md)
- Aircraft profiles and POH import. [Aircraft and fuel](./aircraft-and-fuel.md)
- Fuel plan. [Aircraft and fuel](./aircraft-and-fuel.md)
- Vertical profile of the route. [Virtual Radar](./virtual-radar.md)
- Find a destination. [Aerodromes and briefing](./aerodromes-and-briefing.md)

## Briefing
- Aerodrome card: info, weather, NOTAMs. [Aerodromes and briefing](./aerodromes-and-briefing.md)
- METAR, TAF, cloud profile, wind gauges. [Weather and NOTAMs](./weather-and-notams.md)
- NOTAMs on the map and in lists. [Weather and NOTAMs](./weather-and-notams.md)
- Nearby brief. [Aerodromes and briefing](./aerodromes-and-briefing.md)
- Home airfield.

## Flying
- Flying mode with GPS, in-flight instrument bar, auto zoom, orientation. [Flying mode and alerts](./flying-and-alerts.md)
- Auto start and stop.
- Direct to and drop point.
- Pilot log: live ETA, frequencies. [Flying mode and alerts](./flying-and-alerts.md)
- Alert stack: airspace, obstacles, aerodromes, NOTAMs.
- Vertical profile with your aircraft. [Virtual Radar](./virtual-radar.md)
- Flight logs, review, GPX. [Flight logs](./flight-logs.md)
- Simulators and external simulators. [Simulators and devices](./simulators-and-devices.md)
- Native only: background tracking, barometer, vario, QNH, gauges bar.

## Setup
- Offline data and AIRAC updates. [Offline use](./offline-and-data.md)
- Account, passkeys, sync, delete. [Account and sync](./account-and-sync.md)
- All settings. [Settings reference](./settings.md)
